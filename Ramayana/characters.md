# 🌟 RAMAYANA – MAIN CHARACTERS

## 1. Lord Rama

![Lord Rama](Assets/rama.jpg)

Lord Rama is the central character of the Ramayana. He represents truth, courage, kindness and righteousness.

## 2. Goddess Sita

![Goddess Sita](Assets/sita.jpg)

Sita is the wife of Lord Rama. She represents strength, devotion, patience and purity.

## 3. Ravana

![Ravana](Assets/ravana.jpg)

Ravana is the king of Lanka. He is known for his knowledge, power and intelligence.

## 4. Lord Hanuman

![Lord Hanuman](Assets/hanuman.jpg)

Hanuman is a devoted follower of Lord Rama. He represents loyalty, courage and devotion.

## 5. Lakshman

![Lakshman](Assets/lakshman.jpg)

Lakshman is the younger brother of Lord Rama. He accompanies Rama and Sita during their exile and supports them throughout their journey.
