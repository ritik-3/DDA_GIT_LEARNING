## 🌟 About the Movie

Ramayana is a cinematic adaptation of the timeless Indian epic. It tells the story of Lord Rama, his journey of courage, devotion and righteousness, and his battle against Ravana.

The film explores the eternal victory of Dharma over Adharma.

## 📖 Story Overview

The story follows Prince Rama through exile, sacrifice, loyalty and extraordinary challenges. Alongside Sita and Lakshman, he faces difficult circumstances that test his courage and commitment to Dharma.

---

## 💫 Important Themes

- Dharma and righteousness
- Love and devotion
- Courage and sacrifice
- Loyalty and brotherhood
- Good versus evil
