Mirzapur: The Movie 🎬

Overview

Mirzapur: The Movie is a 2026 Hindi crime-action thriller that
brings the world of the popular Mirzapur franchise to the big screen.
Directed by Gurmmeet Singh, written by Puneet Krishna, and
produced by Ritesh Sidhwani and Farhan Akhtar, the film returns
to the power struggles, rivalries, ambition, and revenge that define the
Mirzapur universe.

The movie was released in theatres on 4 September 2026 in Hindi and
Telugu.

Note: This README contains story details and spoilers.

Story

The film explores an untold chapter from the Mirzapur universe,
revisiting the period when Guddu Pandit and Bablu Pandit become
increasingly involved in Kaleen Bhaiya's criminal empire.

As Guddu and Bablu rise within the power structure, their growing
influence creates tension with Munna Tripathi, Kaleen Bhaiya's
ambitious and unpredictable son. Munna wants recognition and control,
while Guddu and Bablu are becoming important players in the same world.

The story expands the conflict beyond Mirzapur with the arrival of
Babban Babua Yadav, a powerful gangster from Rajasthan. Babban's
ambitions create another threat to Kaleen Bhaiya's authority and push
the major characters into a larger struggle for power.

The film combines family rivalries, political connections, crime,
revenge, and shifting loyalties. At its core, the story is about who can
control the throne of Mirzapur and how far different characters are
willing to go to gain or protect power.

Key Themes

1. Power

The central theme is the fight to control Mirzapur and its criminal
empire.

2. Ambition

Guddu, Munna, Kaleen Bhaiya, and Babban each have different ambitions,
creating constant competition.

3. Family Rivalry

Personal relationships and family loyalty repeatedly collide with the
desire for power.

4. Revenge

Past conflicts influence the decisions of the characters and continue to
shape their relationships.

5. Loyalty and Betrayal

Characters frequently have to decide whether to remain loyal to their
families and alliances or pursue their own interests.

Why the Movie Is Important to the Franchise

The film gives fans a cinematic chapter set within the Mirzapur
universe rather than simply repeating the events of the television
seasons. It brings several familiar characters back together and
introduces new players, particularly Babban Babua Yadav, to expand
the conflict.

The film's story is designed around the franchise's familiar ideas of
power, revenge, family, and the fight for the throne of Mirzapur.

Summary

Mirzapur: The Movie returns to the intense world of the Tripathi and
Pandit families. With Kaleen Bhaiya trying to protect his position,
Munna seeking greater power, Guddu and Bablu becoming increasingly
influential, and Babban Yadav entering the conflict, the movie presents
another battle for control.

It expands the Mirzapur universe while bringing together many of its
most recognizable characters on the big screen.
