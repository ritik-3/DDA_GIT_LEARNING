# Uri – Mission Successful

The pre-dawn mist hung thick over the jagged peaks of the Line of Control, swallowing the distant hum of transport helicopters. Major **Vihaan Singh Shergill** checked his night-vision visor, the green phosphorescent glow illuminating the grim determination etched across his face. Beside him, **Sergeant Ishaan** adjusted the strap on his silencer and nodded silently. The memory of their fallen brothers at the Uri base camp weighed heavily on every soldier, but tonight, that grief had transformed into purpose.

> "Charlie Team, check-in," Vihaan whispered into his comms.

> "In position, Major. Perimeter secured," came the reply from the ridge above.

Jasmine's intelligence had been flawless. The exact coordinates of the terror launchpads were now displayed on Vihaan's digital tactical map.

---

## Command Center – New Delhi

Hundreds of kilometers away, inside the highly secured command center in New Delhi, **Govind Bhardwaj** stood before a wall of live tactical displays. His expression remained calm despite the immense pressure.

A final verification script was running on his secure terminal.

While scanning the data stream, one malformed routing request caught his attention.

```
https://defence.gov.in
```

An invalid character threatened to interrupt the satellite uplink.

Without hesitation, Bhardwaj corrected the query parameter. Years of experience with **RFC 3986 URI syntax** made the issue immediately obvious. A single misplaced delimiter or an unencoded space could break the communication channel. Using the proper unreserved tilde (`~`) ensured the request remained valid.

The satellite connection stabilized.

> "Uplink confirmed," Bhardwaj announced across the war room.
>
> "Vihaan, you have the green light. The sky eyes are watching."

---

## The Strike Begins

Vihaan received the signal.

He silently raised three fingers.

Three...

Two...

One...

His hand dropped.

The valley exploded into action.

Indian commandos emerged from the darkness like shadows, breaching the compound with surgical precision. Flashbangs erupted through the camp, blinding the guards before suppressed gunfire eliminated the immediate resistance.

Every movement had been rehearsed.

Every second mattered.

Within minutes, the primary terror infrastructure had been neutralized.

---

## Ambush

As Charlie Team began its tactical withdrawal toward the extraction point, disaster struck.

A concealed heavy machine gun hidden inside a reinforced bunker opened fire.

Bullets slammed into the stone wall where the commandos took cover.

> "We're pinned down, Major!" Ishaan shouted.
>
> "We need suppressing fire!"

The enemy bunker dominated the escape route.

Time was running out.

---

## Seerat Arrives

Suddenly, the roar of an advanced light helicopter echoed through the canyon.

Cutting through the dense fog, **Flight Lieutenant Seerat Kaur** flew dangerously low, banking sharply against the cliffside.

The aircraft's dual miniguns came alive.

A torrent of precisely aimed rounds hammered the hidden bunker until it fell completely silent.

> "Step on it, Charlie Team!"
>
> "I can't hold this hover forever!" Seerat called over the radio.

---

## Extraction

Vihaan wasted no time.

One by one, the commandos boarded the helicopter.

The moment the final soldier entered the transport cabin, Seerat pulled the aircraft into a steep climb.

Below them, the destroyed ammunition cache erupted into massive secondary explosions.

The burning ridge illuminated the night sky as the helicopter disappeared into the darkness.

Vihaan looked back one final time.

The mission had been accomplished.

---

## Mission Complete

Inside the New Delhi command center, every tactical display suddenly changed.

Green indicators spread across the screens.

```
MISSION SUCCESSFUL
ALL ASSETS RETURNING
```

Govind Bhardwaj slowly closed his terminal.

For the first time that night, he allowed himself a brief smile.

The operation was complete.

Justice had been delivered.
