The Minions have always lived to serve the most evil masters, but they accidentally lose every boss they follow. Feeling hopeless, three Minions—Kevin, Stuart, and Bob—set out on a journey to find a new master. They travel to Villain-Con in the United States, where they meet Scarlet Overkill and become her helpers. Scarlet orders them to steal the British Queen's crown, but things go wrong. In the end, the Minions stop Scarlet's evil plan, and a young Gru appears, becoming the Minions' next master. This sets up the story for the Despicable Me movies.
