# Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle — Music

## Music

The film's soundtrack was composed by **Yuki Kajiura and Go Shiina**, who have also worked on previous *Demon Slayer* anime projects.

The score supports both the large-scale action sequences and the emotional character moments throughout the film.

## Theme Songs

### Taiyō ga Noboranai Sekai

* **Artist:** Aimer
* **Type:** Theme Song

### Zankoku no Yoru ni Kagayake

* **Artist:** LiSA
* **Type:** Theme Song

## Music Highlights

The soundtrack is used throughout the film to build tension during battles and emphasize important emotional scenes.
