# Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle — Director

## Director

**Haruo Sotozaki** directed *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Infinity Castle*.

Sotozaki has directed the *Demon Slayer* anime series and previous *Demon Slayer* theatrical projects.

## Role in the Film

Sotozaki oversaw the direction of the film's dramatic scenes, action sequences and overall cinematic presentation.

The film continues the visual style established throughout the anime while expanding the scale of the Infinity Castle battles for theatrical presentation.

## Production Studio

The movie was produced by **Ufotable**, the animation studio responsible for the *Demon Slayer* anime adaptation.

Ufotable handled the film's animation and visual production, working with the series' established creative team.
