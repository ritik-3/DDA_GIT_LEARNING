# Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Infinity Castle

## Overview

**Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Movie: Infinity Castle** is a 2025 Japanese animated dark-fantasy action film based on Koyoharu Gotouge's *Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* manga. The film is directed by **Haruo Sotozaki** and produced by **Ufotable**.

It is the **first film in a planned trilogy** adapting the final battle of the series. The story continues directly after the fourth season of the anime and brings the Demon Slayer Corps into the Infinity Castle for their confrontation with Muzan Kibutsuji and the Upper Rank demons.

* **Release Date:** July 18, 2025
* **Runtime:** 155 minutes
* **Country:** Japan
* **Language:** Japanese
* **Production Studio:** Ufotable
* **Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Action / Adventure / Animation

---

## Theatrical Experience

*Infinity Castle* was designed as a large-scale theatrical continuation of the *Demon Slayer* anime.

The Infinity Castle provides a constantly changing environment for the battles, allowing the film to move between different locations and characters while maintaining a connection to the larger final battle.

The movie places considerable emphasis on action choreography, animation, visual effects and sound design. These elements are especially prominent during the major battles.

---

## Reception

The film received generally positive critical and audience responses.

Critics particularly discussed the film's:

* Animation quality
* Action sequences
* Visual presentation
* Emotional moments
* Character-focused storytelling
* Adaptation of the manga

Some criticism focused on the **155-minute runtime**, pacing during certain sections, the use of flashbacks, and the fact that the movie does not conclude the entire final battle because it is the first installment of a trilogy.

---

## Box Office

*Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle* became a major international box-office success.

The film performed strongly in Japan and overseas markets and became one of the most commercially successful Japanese animated theatrical releases.

Its strong theatrical performance also reflected the continued popularity of the *Demon Slayer* franchise internationally.

## Review

### What Works

* High-quality animation throughout the major action sequences
* Detailed and dynamic battle choreography
* Strong visual presentation of the Infinity Castle
* Emotional character moments
* Effective combination of action and dramatic storytelling
* Strong theatrical presentation

### What Could Be Better

* The 155-minute runtime can feel lengthy.
* Some flashbacks interrupt the momentum of the action.
* The film does not provide a complete conclusion to the final story.
* Viewers unfamiliar with the previous anime seasons may find some character and story developments difficult to follow.

### Overall Impression

*Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle* functions primarily as a large-scale theatrical continuation of the anime. It combines action-heavy sequences with emotional character moments while beginning the final conflict of the series.

The film is particularly dependent on the viewer's familiarity with the previous *Demon Slayer* story, making it more suitable for viewers who have completed the anime up to the Infinity Castle arc.
