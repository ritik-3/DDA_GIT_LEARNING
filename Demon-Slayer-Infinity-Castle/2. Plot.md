## Plot 

## Major Battles

The film begins after the Demon Slayer Corps' confrontation with Muzan Kibutsuji. Muzan takes the Corps into the mysterious Infinity Castle, a vast and constantly shifting fortress controlled by the demon Nakime.

Separated throughout the castle, the Demon Slayers are forced into individual battles against some of the strongest demons in the Twelve Kizuki.

The film features several major battles inside the Infinity Castle, including Shinobu Kocho vs. Doma, Zenitsu Agatsuma vs. Kaigaku, and Tanjiro Kamado and Giyu Tomioka vs. Akaza.
