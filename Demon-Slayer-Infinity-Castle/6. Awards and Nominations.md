## Major Recognition

The film was particularly recognized for its animation, visual presentation, action sequences, and large-scale theatrical experience. It won **Animation of the Year – Feature Film** at the Tokyo Anime Award Festival and **Best International Animated Film** at the Saturn Awards. 

It also received a nomination for **Best Motion Picture – Animated** at the 2026 Golden Globe Awards. 

## International Recognition

The film's nominations at awards such as the **Golden Globes** and **Producers Guild Awards** showed its recognition beyond anime-specific award ceremonies. 

## Summary

*Infinity Castle* received recognition from both Japanese and international award organizations, particularly for its animation and cinematic presentation.
