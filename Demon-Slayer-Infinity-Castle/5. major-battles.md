# Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle — Major Battles

## Shinobu Kocho vs. Doma

Shinobu Kocho confronts Doma, the Upper Rank Two demon. Their battle is connected to Shinobu's desire for revenge and leads to an important confrontation involving Kanao Tsuyuri.

## Zenitsu Agatsuma vs. Kaigaku

Zenitsu Agatsuma faces Kaigaku, his former fellow student under Jigoro Kuwajima. Their battle forces Zenitsu to confront his past and shows his growth as a Demon Slayer.

## Tanjiro Kamado and Giyu Tomioka vs. Akaza

Tanjiro Kamado and Giyu Tomioka fight Akaza, the Upper Rank Three demon. The battle combines intense sword fighting with Akaza's memories of his human life.

## Akaza's Human Past

During the battle, Akaza's former identity as Hakuji is revealed. His relationship with Keizo and Koyuki provides important emotional context for his character.

## Battle Significance

These battles are important because they combine the final confrontation with the personal histories and motivations of the characters involved.
