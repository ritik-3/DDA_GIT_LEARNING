 Abhay Verma as Bittu

Bittu is a young, sensitive, and introverted boy who lives in Pune with his mother and grandmother. He is a simple person who avoids conflicts and often struggles with confidence. He works at his mother's beauty parlour and has a close bond with his family.

Bittu has been in love with his childhood friend Bela for a long time but never expresses his feelings because he is shy and afraid of losing her friendship. He is caring, emotional, and always thinks about the happiness of others before himself.

When he accidentally awakens the evil spirit Munjya, his peaceful life changes completely. At first, Bittu is scared and confused because he has never faced anything supernatural before. But as Munjya starts threatening Bela and his family, Bittu discovers his inner strength.


Character Traits:

Kind-hearted and emotional
Shy but brave when needed
Loyal towards family and friends
Innocent and humble
Protective of the people he loves


Character Journey:
Bittu's journey is about transforming from a fearful and uncertain person into someone who accepts responsibility and fights against evil to protect his loved ones.
