Sathyaraj as Padri Elvis Karim Prabhakar

Padri Elvis Karim Prabhakar is an experienced priest who understands supernatural forces. He helps Bittu and his family discover the truth about Munjya and guides them in fighting the evil spirit.

He is calm, intelligent, and fearless. Unlike others who panic after seeing supernatural events, Elvis remains composed and uses his knowledge to handle the situation.

His character adds seriousness and wisdom to the horror-comedy story.


Character Traits:

Wise and knowledgeable
Fearless and calm
Experienced in supernatural matters
Logical and observant
Helpful and compassionate


Character Journey:
Padri Elvis acts as a guide and mentor for Bittu, helping him understand the danger and find the courage to fight against Munjya.
