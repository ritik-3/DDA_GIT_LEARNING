 Sharvari as Bela

Bela is a confident, independent, and modern young woman who shares a strong friendship with Bittu since childhood. She is cheerful, adventurous, and enjoys living life freely.

Unlike Bittu, Bela is more expressive and confident. She does not hesitate to speak her mind and follows her own choices. She is unaware that Bittu secretly loves her, but she values their friendship deeply.

When Munjya becomes interested in her, Bela unknowingly becomes the center of the supernatural conflict. She faces the danger with courage and becomes an important part of Bittu's emotional growth.


Character Traits:

Confident and independent
Strong-minded and fearless
Friendly and caring
Positive and energetic
Emotionally mature


Character Journey:
Bela represents strength and courage. Her character shows that she is not just a person who needs saving; she actively faces challenges and supports Bittu during difficult situations.
