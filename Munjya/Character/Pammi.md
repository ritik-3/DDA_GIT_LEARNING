 Mona Singh as Pammi

Pammi is Bittu's loving and protective mother. She runs a beauty parlour and takes care of her family. She is a strong woman who tries to support Bittu in every situation.

Pammi shares a close relationship with her son and understands his emotions. She worries about Bittu because she notices strange changes happening around him after Munjya's arrival.

She represents a typical Indian mother who is caring, practical, and emotionally connected to her child.


Character Traits:

Loving and protective
Strong and responsible
Family-oriented
Practical and supportive
Emotionally sensitive


Character Journey:
Pammi's role highlights the importance of family support. She stands by Bittu when he faces fear and danger.
