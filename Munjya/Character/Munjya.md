 Munjya (CGI Character)


Munjya is a supernatural spirit inspired by Indian folklore, especially the belief around spirits of people who die with unfulfilled desires. The character is based on the story of a young boy named Gotya, whose obsession with marrying Munni leads him toward dark rituals.

After his death, Gotya becomes Munjya — a ghost trapped between worlds because his biggest desire was never fulfilled. He is small in appearance but extremely powerful and dangerous.

Munjya is a unique villain because he is not only scary but also has a childish and humorous personality. His obsession, stubbornness, and emotional immaturity make him unpredictable.

He targets Bittu because of his family's connection to his past and later becomes obsessed with Bela, believing she can help him complete his unfinished wish.


Character Traits:

Mischievous and unpredictable
Stubborn and obsessive
Childlike but dangerous
Emotional and possessive
Funny yet terrifying


Character Journey:
Munjya's character represents how an unfulfilled desire and obsession can turn into something destructive. His entire existence revolves around completing the wish he could not achieve during his lifetime.
