## 📖 About the Movie

Train to Busan is a South Korean action-horror thriller directed by Yeon Sang-ho.

The story follows passengers traveling from Seoul to Busan when a sudden zombie outbreak takes place on the train. The passengers must fight for survival and protect each other.
