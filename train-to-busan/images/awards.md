## 🎖️ Important Nominations

### 2016 — Blue Dragon Film Awards

*Train to Busan* was nominated for:

* Best Film
* Best Supporting Actor — Kim Eui-sung
* Best Supporting Actor — Ma Dong-seok
* Best Supporting Actress — Jung Yu-mi
* Best Art Direction
* Best Editing
* Best Cinematography
* Best Lighting
* Best Screenplay

It won the Technical Award and Audience Choice Award.

### 2017 — Asian Film Awards

The film received **5 nominations**:

* Best Actor — Gong Yoo
* Best Supporting Actor — Ma Dong-seok
* Best Editor — Yang Jin-mo
* Best Visual Effects — Jung Hwang-su
* Best Costume Designer — Kwon Yoo-jin & Rim Seung-hee

It did not win these categories.

### 2017 — Saturn Awards

* Best Horror Film — **Nominated** 🎖️

### 2017 — AACTA Awards

* Best Asian Film — **Nominated**

## 📌 Quick Summary

**Train to Busan (2016)** was recognized both in South Korea and internationally, particularly for its **direction, special effects, acting, horror elements, and emotional storytelling**. KOFIC's official film database records its awards and festival appearances.
