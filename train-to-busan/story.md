# 🚆 Train to Busan — Story

*Train to Busan* is a South Korean action-horror thriller about a father and his young daughter who travel by train from Seoul to Busan.

During their journey, a mysterious zombie outbreak suddenly spreads across the country. The passengers on the train must fight to survive as the infected become increasingly dangerous.

Seok-woo, the girl's father, tries to protect his daughter while facing difficult situations and making sacrifices. Along the way, they meet other passengers, including Sang-hwa and his pregnant wife Seong-kyeong, who help them survive.

As the train travels toward Busan, the passengers face fear, betrayal, courage, and loss. The journey becomes more than just an attempt to escape the zombies—it becomes a test of humanity and self-sacrifice.

## 🎬 Main Themes

* 👨‍👧 Father and daughter relationship
* 🤝 Teamwork and survival
* ❤️ Love and sacrifice
* 🧟 Zombie outbreak
* 💪 Courage and humanity

## ⭐ My Review

*Train to Busan* combines thrilling action with an emotional story about family, courage, and sacrifice. It is more than a zombie movie because it also shows how people behave when their lives are at risk.
