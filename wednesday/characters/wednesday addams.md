# Wednesday Addams

Wednesday Addams is the central character of the Netflix series Wednesday. She is the intelligent, mysterious, independent, and dark-humoured daughter of Gomez and Morticia Addams.

She attends Nevermore Academy, a school for outcasts, where she discovers her psychic abilities and becomes involved in mysterious events, supernatural secrets, and dangerous investigations.

Wednesday is known for her pale appearance, black clothing, signature braided hair, emotionless expressions, sharp sarcasm, strong personality, and unusual interest in mysteries and the macabre.

**Character Details:**
- Full Name: Wednesday Friday Addams
- Portrayed By: Jenna Ortega
- Family: Addams Family
- Parents: Gomez Addams and Morticia Addams
- Brother: Pugsley Addams
- School: Nevermore Academy
- Personality: Intelligent, sarcastic, independent, observant, fearless, and mysterious
- Special Ability: Psychic visions
- Best Friend: Enid Sinclair
- Main Interests: Mystery, investigation, writing, fencing, and the supernatural

Wednesday's character represents individuality, independence, intelligence, and the courage to embrace one's uniqueness.
