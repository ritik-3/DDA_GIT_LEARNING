Wednesday is an American supernatural mystery-comedy television series developed by Alfred Gough and Miles Millar. Based on the iconic Addams Family character Wednesday Addams, the series follows her journey at Nevermore Academy, a school for supernatural outcasts.

The story revolves around Wednesday's mysterious adventures, psychic abilities, unusual friendships, family secrets, and investigations into supernatural events. With its gothic atmosphere, dark humour, suspense, and intriguing characters, the series presents a unique blend of mystery, fantasy, and comedy.
