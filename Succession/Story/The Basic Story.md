The Basic Story

Imagine a billionaire family where:

Money = Power
Family = Business
Business = Family

That's essentially Succession.

Logan Roy built Waystar RoyCo into a massive media empire.

He has four children:

Connor Roy
Kendall Roy
Roman Roy
Siobhan "Shiv" Roy

Each child has a different personality and relationship with their father.

The central question is:

"Who will succeed Logan Roy?"

But Logan doesn't simply hand the company to one of them.

Instead, the possibility of succession becomes a constant source of competition, manipulation, betrayal and reconciliation.
