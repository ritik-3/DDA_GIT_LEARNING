# Directors and Creative Team

## Director

**Name:** Shinsuke Sato

Shinsuke Sato is the director of Alice in Borderland. He is responsible for directing the series and bringing its survival games, characters, and storyline to the screen.

## Original Creator

**Name:** Haro Aso

Haro Aso is the creator of the original manga on which Alice in Borderland is based.

## Screenplay

The screenplay was developed by the writing team led by Shinsuke Sato and Yasuko Kuramitsu.
