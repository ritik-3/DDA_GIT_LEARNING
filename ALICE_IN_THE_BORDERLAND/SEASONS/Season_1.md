## 1. Season Overview

- **Series:** Alice in Borderland
- **Season:** 1

- **Number of Episodes:** 8
- **Language:** Japanese
- **Genre:** Survival, Science Fiction, Thriller, Mystery
- **Director:** Shinsuke Sato
- **Based On:** Alice in Borderland manga by Haro Aso
- **Streaming Platform:** Netflix

Season 1 introduces viewers to the mysterious world of the Borderland, where people must participate in dangerous games to survive. Each game is represented by a playing card, and players must clear challenges to extend their visas.

## 2. Storyline

The story begins in Tokyo, where Ryohei Arisu, a young man who enjoys video games, spends time with his two close friends, Daikichi Karube and Chota Segawa.

After an unexpected incident in Shibuya, the three friends find themselves in a strangely empty version of Tokyo. They soon discover that they have entered a mysterious place called the Borderland.

In this world, people must participate in deadly games. Each game has a playing card that indicates its difficulty and category. Players who fail to complete a game are eliminated.

Arisu and his friends try to understand the rules of this world while searching for a way to return home. Along the journey, Arisu meets Usagi, a skilled climber who becomes an important companion.

As the story progresses, the players discover a place called the Beach, where a group of survivors are collecting playing cards in the hope of completing the entire deck.

However, the Beach is not the safe place it initially appears to be. Conflicts, betrayal, and a deadly game change everything.

## 3. Understanding the Game System

Every game in the Borderland is represented by a playing card.

### Card Suits and Their Meanings

| Card Suit | Game Category | Main Skill Tested |
|---|---|---|
| Clubs ♣ | Teamwork | Cooperation and coordination |
| Diamonds ♦ | Intelligence | Logic and strategy |
| Spades ♠ | Physical | Strength and endurance |
| Hearts ♥ | Psychological | Trust, emotions and betrayal |

### Number Cards

The number on a card represents the difficulty level of the game. A higher number generally indicates a more difficult challenge.

### Visas

Every player receives a visa that indicates how many days they can remain in the Borderland.

- Clearing a game extends the player's visa.
- Failing to clear a game results in elimination.
- If a player's visa expires, a laser from the sky eliminates them.

## 4. Important Games in Season 1

### 1. Three of Clubs – Dead or Alive

- **Category:** Clubs
- **Type:** Teamwork and decision-making
- **Participants:** Arisu, Karube, Chota and other players

Players must choose between different doors while trying to identify the correct route before time runs out.

The game tests observation, teamwork and the ability to make decisions under pressure.

### 2. Five of Spades – Tag

- **Category:** Spades
- **Type:** Physical survival
- **Location:** Apartment building

Players must survive against armed attackers while trying to reach a safe room.

The challenge tests speed, physical ability and survival instincts.

### 3. Seven of Hearts – Hide and Seek

- **Category:** Hearts
- **Type:** Psychological and emotional survival

Players are assigned different roles in a dangerous game of hide-and-seek.

The game forces participants to make emotionally painful decisions and tests friendship, trust and sacrifice.

### 4. Four of Clubs – Distance

- **Category:** Clubs
- **Type:** Teamwork and endurance

Players must travel through a dangerous route while following the game's instructions.

The challenge requires cooperation, observation and physical endurance.

### 5. Four of Diamonds – Light Bulb Game

- **Category:** Diamonds
- **Type:** Intelligence and logical reasoning

Players must determine which switch controls a light bulb by using logical deduction.

This game demonstrates the importance of analytical thinking rather than physical strength.

### 6. Ten of Hearts – Witch Hunt

- **Category:** Hearts
- **Type:** Psychological and social manipulation
- **Location:** The Beach

Players must identify the witch responsible for a murder and throw the body into a fire.

The game creates panic and suspicion among the Beach members, causing them to turn against one another.

It becomes one of the most important turning points of the season.

- **Usagi:** A skilled climber who becomes Arisu's companion.

- **Kuina:** A skilled fighter who becomes an important ally.

## 6. The Beach

The Beach is a hotel that serves as a gathering place for many Borderland survivors.

Its members believe that collecting all the playing cards will help them escape the Borderland.

Important features of the Beach:

- It has a structured leadership system.
- Members work together to collect cards.
- The organization has strict rules.
- Different groups compete for power.
- Its leadership becomes increasingly unstable.

The Beach plays a major role in the development of the season's story.

## 7. Major Events and Turning Points

1. Arisu, Karube and Chota enter the Borderland.
2. The three friends experience their first deadly games.
3. Arisu discovers the rules of the visa system.
4. Arisu meets Usagi and other survivors.
5. The Beach becomes a central location in the story.
6. Players begin questioning the purpose of collecting cards.
7. The Witch Hunt creates chaos and destroys the Beach's unity.
8. The surviving players discover that another stage of the games is about to begin.

## 8. Season Ending Explained

After the Witch Hunt, the survivors discover that collecting the numbered cards was only the first stage.

A new phase begins when mysterious airships display the Face Cards.

Mira appears on a screen and announces that the players must now participate in another set of games.

This revelation establishes the foundation for Season 2.

## 9. Major Themes

- Friendship and sacrifice
- Survival and human instinct
- Trust and betrayal
- Intelligence versus physical strength
- The value of human life
- Psychological pressure
- The desire to return home

## 10. Interesting Facts

- The series is adapted from the manga written by Haro Aso.
- Shinsuke Sato directed the live-action adaptation.
- The Borderland is presented as an alternate version of Tokyo.
- The four card suits represent different types of challenges.
- The story combines survival games with psychological and philosophical questions.

## 11. Personal Takeaway

Season 1 introduces the central concept of Alice in Borderland and demonstrates that survival requires more than physical strength. Intelligence, teamwork, emotional control and trust can all determine whether a person survives.
