Season 3 continues the story after Arisu and Usagi return to the real world.

This season introduces the final card, the Joker, and presents a different kind of challenge through a series of tournament-style games.

## 2. Storyline

Five years after the events of Season 2, Arisu and Usagi are married and living in the real world.

Although they have no clear memories of their experiences in the Borderland, they sometimes experience strange dreams and memories.

Their peaceful life changes when Usagi becomes involved with Ryuji, a university professor who is fascinated by near-death experiences and the afterlife.

Ryuji's research is connected to Banda, who has chosen to remain in the Borderland as a Citizen.

When Usagi is taken back into the Borderland, Arisu becomes desperate to find her.

With Ann's help, Arisu returns to the Borderland by temporarily stopping his heart in the real world.

He discovers that the Joker is the only remaining card and that he must face a new series of deadly challenges to find Usagi.

## 3. Understanding the Joker Stage

The Joker represents the final stage of the Borderland's card system.

Unlike the previous stages, the Joker games are organized as a tournament.

Players must face a sequence of increasingly dangerous challenges involving:

- Intelligence and quick thinking
- Teamwork and cooperation
- Physical endurance
- Trust and loyalty
- Decision-making under pressure

The participants must complete the games to reach the final challenge and determine whether they can return to the real world.

## 4. Important Games in Season 3

### 1. Sacred Fortunes

- **Type:** Logic and interpretation
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players must interpret fortune messages and make decisions based on the clues they receive.

The game tests their ability to understand information and respond under pressure.

### 2. Zombie Hunt

- **Type:** Team strategy and survival
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players participate in a competition involving different teams and changing roles.

They must use strategy and cooperation to survive the challenge.

### 3. Rhythm

- **Type:** Physical reaction and timing
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players must react to incoming laser attacks and move according to the rhythm and direction of the hazards.

The game tests timing, concentration and physical coordination.

### 4. Runaway Train

- **Type:** Survival and problem-solving
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players must navigate a dangerous train containing multiple carriages and poisonous gas chambers.

They must identify a safe route and find a way to stop the train before time runs out.

### 5. Kick the Can

- **Type:** Teamwork and physical survival
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players participate in a dangerous version of the familiar playground game.

They must manage the can, avoid elimination and work together while facing unexpected dangers.

### 6. Tokyo Bingo Tower

- **Type:** Physical endurance and coordination
- **Stage:** Joker tournament

Players must climb Tokyo Tower and complete a Bingo challenge.

They face dangerous obstacles, including cannonballs, while attempting to complete the required pattern.

### 7. Future Sugoroku

- **Type:** Logic, probability and teamwork
- **Stage:** Final game

The final challenge takes place in a maze made up of a 5 × 5 grid of rooms.

Players must roll dice to determine which doors they can use and navigate through the rooms to find the exit.

Each player begins with 15 points. Moving through doors and staying behind can cost points, while some rooms impose additional penalties.

The players must manage their points carefully because reaching zero results in elimination.

The game also shows possible futures behind different doors, forcing players to consider the consequences of their decisions.

## 7. The Final Game Explained

The final game, Future Sugoroku, is based on a grid of rooms.

Its major rules include:

- The game takes place across a 5 × 5 grid.
- Players roll dice to determine how many participants can pass through each coloured door.
- Players begin with 15 points.
- Moving through a door costs one point.
- Staying behind also costs one point.
- Certain rooms impose additional point penalties.
- Players are eliminated if their points reach zero.

Each room can reveal a possible future, making the game both a test of logic and a psychological challenge.

The players must cooperate and carefully manage their points to reach the exit.

## 8. Season Ending Explained

During the final game, Arisu tries to help the group reach the exit together.

However, the players face difficult decisions, and not everyone survives.

Arisu eventually chooses to sacrifice himself so that Usagi and the others can return to the real world.

After several dangerous events, Arisu and Usagi manage to find each other again.

The Watchman explains that the Joker is not simply another human game host. It represents a larger part of the Borderland's mysterious system.

Arisu is offered a choice between returning to the world of the living or entering the world of the dead.

He chooses life because he wants to be with Usagi.

Usagi also finds the strength to move forward after confronting the emotional pain connected to her father.

Both of them return to the real world, where they begin preparing for their future together.

The final scene moves to the United States and shows a waitress whose name tag reads "Alice". This leaves the audience with a suggestion that the mystery of the Borderland may extend beyond Japan.
