# Morizono Aguni's Friend - Hatter

![Hatter](10_hatter.jpg)

- **Actor:** Nobuaki Kaneko
- **Role:** Founder of the Beach
- **Personality:** Charismatic, ambitious and influential.
- **Skills:** Leadership and persuasion.

## Description
Hatter is the founder of the Beach, a community created by players who aim to collect all the playing cards. He presents the Beach as a place where survivors can live together.
