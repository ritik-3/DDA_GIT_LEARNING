# Chota Segawa

![Chota](04_chota.jpg)

- **Actor:** Yuki Morinaga
- **Role:** Supporting Character
- **Personality:** Kind, sensitive and loyal.
- **Skills:** Teamwork and cooperation.

## Description
Chota works at an IT company and is one of Arisu's closest friends. He enters the Borderland alongside Arisu and Karube and faces the challenges of this mysterious world.
