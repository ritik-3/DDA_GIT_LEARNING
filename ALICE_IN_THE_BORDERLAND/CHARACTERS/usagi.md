# Yuzuha Usagi

![Usagi](02_usagi.jpg)

- **Actor:** Tao Tsuchiya
- **Role:** Main Protagonist
- **Personality:** Brave, independent and compassionate.
- **Skills:** Climbing, agility and physical endurance.

## Description
Usagi is an experienced mountain climber who relies on her physical abilities and survival instincts. She meets Arisu in the Borderland and becomes an important companion in his journey.
