# Suguru Niragi

![Niragi](07_niragi.jpg)

- **Actor:** Dori Sakurada
- **Role:** Antagonistic Character
- **Personality:** Aggressive, unpredictable and intimidating.
- **Skills:** Combat and manipulation.

## Description
Niragi is a dangerous player who becomes involved with the militant faction of the Beach. His aggressive behaviour and unpredictable decisions create serious conflicts.
