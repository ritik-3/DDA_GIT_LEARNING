# Daikichi Karube

![Karube](05_karube.jpg)

- **Actor:** Keita Machida
- **Role:** Supporting Character
- **Personality:** Brave, loyal and protective.
- **Skills:** Fighting and physical strength.

## Description
Karube is one of Arisu's closest friends. He works as a bartender and is known for his courage and willingness to protect the people close to him.
