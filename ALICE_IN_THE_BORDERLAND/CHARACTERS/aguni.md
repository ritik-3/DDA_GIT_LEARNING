# Morizono Aguni

![Aguni](11_aguni.jpg)

- **Actor:** Sho Aoyagi
- **Role:** Major Supporting Character
- **Personality:** Strong, serious and conflicted.
- **Skills:** Fighting and leadership.

## Description
Aguni is a powerful fighter and one of the important figures at the Beach. He leads the militant faction and has a complicated relationship with Hatter.
