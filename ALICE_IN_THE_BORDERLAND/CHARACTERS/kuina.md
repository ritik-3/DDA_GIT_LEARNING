# Hikari Kuina

![Kuina](06_kuina.jpg)

- **Actor:** Aya Asahina
- **Role:** Supporting Character
- **Personality:** Strong, confident and loyal.
- **Skills:** Martial arts and combat.

## Description
Kuina is a courageous player with excellent fighting abilities. She becomes an important ally to Chishiya and other players while navigating the dangerous games.
