# Shuntaro Chishiya

![Chishiya](03_chishiya.jpg)

- **Actor:** Nijiro Murakami
- **Role:** Strategic Player
- **Personality:** Calm, mysterious and calculating.
- **Skills:** Analysis, observation and strategy.

## Description
Chishiya is an intelligent and mysterious player who prefers using logic and strategy instead of physical strength. He is interested in discovering the secrets behind the Borderland.
