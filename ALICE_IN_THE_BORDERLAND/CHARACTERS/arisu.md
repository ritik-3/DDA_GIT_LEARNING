# Ryohei Arisu

![Arisu](01_arisu.jpg)

- **Actor:** Kento Yamazaki
- **Role:** Main Protagonist
- **Personality:** Intelligent, observant and emotional.
- **Skills:** Logical thinking and problem-solving.

## Description
Arisu is a video-game enthusiast who enters the mysterious Borderland with his friends. His gaming knowledge helps him understand complicated rules and develop strategies to survive dangerous games.
