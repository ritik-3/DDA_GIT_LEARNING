
**Movie Name:** Baahubali\
**Director:** S. S. Rajamouli\
**Main Actors:** Prabhas, Rana Daggubati, Anushka Shetty, Tamannaah Bhatia, Ramya Krishnan and Sathyaraj\
**Genre:** Action, Drama, Fantasy and Historical Fiction\
**Languages:** Originally Telugu and Tamil, with dubbed versions in Hindi and other languages\
**Parts:** Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017)

INTRODUCTION

Baahubali is one of the most popular and successful Indian film franchises, directed by S. S. Rajamouli. The movie presents an extraordinary story of courage, love, loyalty, betrayal, revenge, sacrifice and the struggle for power. It is set in the fictional kingdom of Mahishmati, where royal families compete for the throne. The film combines Indian mythology-inspired storytelling, powerful characters, impressive action sequences and advanced visual effects. The story is divided into two parts, Baahubali: The Beginning and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion.

STORY OF BAahUBALI: THE BEGINNING

The story begins with a mysterious woman carrying a small baby while escaping from enemies. She reaches a river and makes a great sacrifice to save the child's life. The baby is rescued by people living near the river and is raised by them. The child is named Shivudu.

As Shivudu grows up, he becomes a strong, brave and adventurous young man. He is always curious about the world beyond his village and dreams of climbing a massive waterfall. After several attempts, he finally reaches the top of the waterfall. There, he discovers a beautiful woman named Avanthika, who belongs to a group of rebels fighting against the cruel ruler Bhallaladeva.

Shivudu falls in love with Avanthika and learns about her mission to rescue Devasena, who has been imprisoned by Bhallaladeva for many years. Shivudu decides to help her and enters the kingdom of Mahishmati.

In Mahishmati, Shivudu discovers that he looks exactly like Amarendra Baahubali, a legendary warrior and former royal heir. People are shocked to see him because they remember Baahubali as a great leader who was loved by the citizens.

Gradually, Shivudu discovers his true identity and learns that he is the son of Amarendra Baahubali. His journey becomes a mission to understand his father's past and fight against injustice.

The first part ends with a shocking revelation when Kattappa, the loyal royal warrior, explains that he killed Amarendra Baahubali. This creates suspense and makes the audience eager to watch the second part.

STORY OF BAahUBALI 2: THE CONCLUSION

The second part explains the complete history of Amarendra Baahubali and reveals the reasons behind his death.

Amarendra Baahubali and Bhallaladeva are cousins who grow up in the royal family of Mahishmati. Both are powerful warriors and candidates for the throne. However, they have completely different personalities. Amarendra Baahubali is kind, responsible and respectful towards ordinary people, while Bhallaladeva is ambitious, jealous and hungry for power.

During their journey to different regions, Amarendra Baahubali meets Devasena, a brave and independent princess. They fall in love with each other. However, their relationship creates problems because Sivagami, the queen of Mahishmati, has already decided that Devasena should marry Bhallaladeva.

Devasena refuses to accept this decision because she wants to marry Amarendra Baahubali. This creates tension between the royal family members. Bhallaladeva becomes jealous and starts planning against Amarendra.

Although Amarendra is the rightful choice for leadership, Bhallaladeva uses political manipulation to turn the royal family against him. Sivagami makes a difficult decision and removes Amarendra from his royal position.

Despite losing his royal status, Amarendra continues to serve the people. He marries Devasena and lives a simple life. However, Bhallaladeva cannot tolerate his popularity and influence.

Bhallaladeva creates a situation in which Kattappa is ordered to kill Amarendra Baahubali. Kattappa is bound by his duty and loyalty to the throne. Even though he loves and respects Amarendra, he follows the royal command and kills him.

Later, Sivagami realizes the truth about Bhallaladeva's intentions. She tries to save Amarendra's son, Mahendra Baahubali, but is forced to escape from the palace. She sacrifices her life while saving the baby.

The story then returns to the present, where Mahendra Baahubali learns everything about his father's sacrifice. He decides to fight Bhallaladeva and free Mahishmati from his cruel rule.

With the support of the people, Devasena, Kattappa and the rebel army, Mahendra attacks the kingdom. A massive battle takes place between the two sides. Finally, Mahendra defeats Bhallaladeva and takes control of Mahishmati. The people celebrate the return of justice and freedom.

MAIN CHARACTERS AND THEIR ROLES

1\. Amarendra Baahubali: He is the central hero of the story. He represents courage, honesty, leadership and sacrifice. He believes that a king's first responsibility is to protect his people.

2\. Mahendra Baahubali: He is the son of Amarendra Baahubali. He represents hope, revenge and the restoration of justice. He fights against Bhallaladeva to reclaim his family's kingdom.

3\. Bhallaladeva: He is the main antagonist. His character represents jealousy, greed, selfishness and the misuse of power. His desire to become king destroys his relationships and ultimately leads to his downfall.

4\. Devasena: She is a brave princess and the wife of Amarendra Baahubali. She represents independence, dignity, confidence and self-respect. She never accepts injustice.

5\. Sivagami: She is the powerful queen of Mahishmati. She represents authority, responsibility and royal tradition. However, her decisions are influenced by manipulation, which creates tragedy.

6\. Kattappa: He is a loyal warrior who serves the royal family. His character represents duty, loyalty and sacrifice. His decision to kill Amarendra becomes the biggest mystery in the first film.

7\. Avanthika: She is a brave warrior who fights against Bhallaladeva's dictatorship. She helps Shivudu understand his purpose and supports the rebellion.

MAJOR THEMES AND MESSAGES

The Baahubali franchise communicates several important life lessons.

Leadership: A good leader should care about people rather than personal power. Amarendra Baahubali demonstrates that leadership is based on responsibility and service.

Courage: Courage means standing against injustice, even when the opponent is stronger.

Loyalty: Kattappa demonstrates the importance and complexity of loyalty and duty.

Jealousy: Bhallaladeva's jealousy destroys his relationships and becomes the main reason for his downfall.

Self-respect: Devasena teaches that people should never sacrifice their dignity and values for power or comfort.

Justice: The story shows that injustice and oppression cannot continue forever. Truth and courage can bring positive change.

CINEMATOGRAPHY AND TECHNICAL ACHIEVEMENTS

One of the biggest strengths of Baahubali is its visual presentation. The film features impressive cinematography, large-scale battle sequences, detailed costumes, royal palaces and beautiful landscapes. The fictional kingdom of Mahishmati is presented with remarkable detail.

The visual effects make the waterfall, massive armies, elephants and battlefield sequences look spectacular. The background music composed by M. M. Keeravani adds emotion and intensity to important scenes.

S. S. Rajamouli's direction successfully combines emotional storytelling with grand action sequences. The performances of Prabhas, Rana Daggubati, Ramya Krishnan, Anushka Shetty and Sathyaraj make the characters memorable.

CONCLUSION

Baahubali is not simply an action and fantasy movie; it is a powerful story about leadership, family, love, sacrifice, loyalty, betrayal and justice. The two films successfully connect the past and present through a story filled with suspense and emotional moments.

The greatest lesson of Baahubali is that true greatness does not come from wealth, royal status or physical strength. It comes from honesty, courage, responsibility and the ability to protect others.

Amarendra Baahubali represents the qualities of an ideal leader, while Bhallaladeva represents the consequences of greed and uncontrolled ambition. Mahendra Baahubali continues his father's legacy by fighting for justice and freedom.

Overall, Baahubali is an outstanding example of Indian filmmaking. Its powerful storytelling, memorable characters, impressive visual effects, emotional music and grand action sequences make it an unforgettable cinematic experience.# **Baahubali – Complete Movie Description (Approximately 1000 Words)**
