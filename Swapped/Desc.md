# Swapped (2026)

## Overview

**Swapped** is an animated fantasy adventure film that follows the journey of **Ollie**, a small Pookoo creature, and **Ivy**, a Javan bird, who belong to two different species with a history of conflict. When a mysterious magical pod causes them to accidentally swap bodies, they are forced to understand each other's worlds and work together to survive.

As they travel through the Valley searching for a way to return to their original forms, Ollie and Ivy discover the importance of trust, friendship, and cooperation. Their adventure becomes even more dangerous when they encounter **Boogle**, a strange and friendly creature who helps guide them, but hides a dark secret connected to the terrifying **Firewolf**.

The film explores themes of empathy, acceptance, and how understanding others can bring different communities together. 

---

## Story

Ollie, a curious and kind-hearted Pookoo, accidentally discovers a magical pod that changes his form and swaps him with Ivy, a Javan bird. Unable to return to their original bodies, the two unlikely companions begin a journey across the Valley to find another pod.

During their adventure, they meet **Boogle**, a seemingly harmless and helpful creature who guides them through unknown dangers. However, they soon discover that Boogle is connected to the legendary **Firewolf**, a powerful creature with a destructive past and a desire to take control of the Valley.

Facing challenges, secrets, and conflicts between different species, Ollie and Ivy must work together to protect their homes and restore balance to the Valley.

---

## Main Characters

### Ollie

Ollie is the main protagonist of the film. He is a young Pookoo known for his curiosity, creativity, and determination. After swapping bodies with Ivy, he learns to see the world from a different perspective and understands the importance of cooperation between different species. 

### Ivy

Ivy is a Javan bird and Ollie's closest companion. Initially part of a species that conflicts with the Pookoo, Ivy is caring, optimistic, and brave. Through her journey with Ollie, she learns the value of friendship and unity beyond differences.

### Boogle / Firewolf

Boogle appears as a friendly and helpful creature who assists Ollie and Ivy during their journey. However, he is later revealed to be the dangerous Firewolf, a powerful antagonist with a painful past and a desire for revenge against the Valley's creatures.
