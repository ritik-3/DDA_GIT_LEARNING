Ollie is the main character of *Swapped*. He is a Pookoo, a small and curious creature who wants to protect his community. As a child, his kindness unintentionally causes conflict between the Pookoos and the Javans, which makes him grow up feeling guilty and distrustful of outsiders. However, after magically swapping species with Ivy, he experiences life from the Javan's perspective and learns to understand their struggles. This journey helps him realize that cooperation is far better than fear and misunderstanding. Ollie is intelligent, creative, and determined, and he never gives up when facing challenges. He even risks his own safety to save Ivy and protect the entire valley. By the end of the story, he grows into a true leader who brings the two communities together. Overall, Ollie represents growth, responsibility, empathy, and selfless leadership.
