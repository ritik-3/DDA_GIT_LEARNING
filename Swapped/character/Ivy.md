Ivy is one of the main character in *Swapped*. She is a Javan, a bird-like creature who deeply cares about her family and flock. Ivy is brave, optimistic, and always willing to help others, even strangers. At the beginning of the story, she sees the Pookoos as rivals because both species are struggling to survive. However, after magically swapping species with Ollie, she experiences life from his perspective. This journey teaches her empathy, understanding, and the importance of looking beyond prejudice. Throughout the story, Ivy remains calm and supportive during difficult situations and encourages Ollie to trust others and never lose hope. Her kindness and compassion eventually help unite the two species that were once enemies. Overall, Ivy represents compassion, courage, forgiveness, and the power of understanding others.
