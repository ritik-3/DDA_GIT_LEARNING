Boogle first appears in *Swapped* as a funny, friendly, and lonely purple fish. He helps Ollie and Ivy by guiding them to the magical transformation pods and seems harmless, humorous, and slightly absent-minded. Because of his cheerful personality, the audience believes he is one of their trusted companions. However, the story takes a shocking turn when it is revealed that Boogle is actually the Firewolf, the main antagonist. He had been pretending to be friendly so he could regain his original powerful form. Once transformed back into the Firewolf, he becomes dangerous and attempts to destroy the valley. His character demonstrates that appearances can be misleading and that hidden motives can have serious consequences. Overall, Boogle represents pain, revenge, and the destructive effects of hatred, making him one of the biggest plot twists in the film.
