Golmaal 3 is a comedy film directed by Rohit Shetty and is the third installment in the popular Golmaal series. The story follows two rival groups of siblings who constantly prank and fight each other until their parents decide to reunite. Their hilarious attempts to stop the marriage lead to a series of funny misunderstandings and chaotic adventures. The movie is known for its slapstick humor, entertaining performances, and family-friendly comedy
