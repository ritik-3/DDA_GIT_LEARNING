UNIQUE_FACTS

1. Thor: The Dark World is the eighth movie in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).

2. The film was directed by Alan Taylor, who also worked on the popular TV series
   Game of Thrones.

3. The streets of Asgard were created as the biggest set ever built for a Marvel
   film at that time.

4. Around 40 Dark Elf costumes were designed and created with the help of approximately 
   100 technicians over three and a half months.

5. Actors Christopher Eccleston and Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje had to memorize dialogues 
   in a special fictional language created for the Dark Elves.

6. The filmmakers travelled to Iceland to capture volcanic landscapes for the Dark World 
   scenes.

7. An aerial camera crew filmed Dettifoss Waterfall in Iceland to help create the waterfalls
   surrounding Asgard using visual effects.

8. Filming took place at the famous Stonehenge monument in England. The crew had to follow
   strict rules and shoot before the site opened to visitors.

9. Before helicopter filming in Greenwich, England, the production team distributed
   approximately 4,000 letters to inform local residents.

10. The movie combines real locations, practical sets, costumes, and computer-generated
    visual effects to create its fantasy worlds.

11. Loki's character plays an important role in the movie, showing his complicated 
    relationship with Thor.

12. The movie explores several of the Nine Realms, giving viewers a deeper look into 
    the Marvel cosmic universe.
