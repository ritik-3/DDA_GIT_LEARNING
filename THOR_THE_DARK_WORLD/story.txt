STORY

After the events of The Avengers, Thor returns to Asgard to restore peace
across the Nine Realms. Meanwhile, an ancient enemy named Malekith, the leader 
of the Dark Elves, awakens after thousands of years. He plans to use a powerful 
ancient weapon called the Aether to destroy the universe and bring eternal darkness.

During a scientific investigation, Jane Foster accidentally comes into contact 
with the Aether, which puts her life in danger. Thor takes Jane to Asgard to 
protect her and find a way to remove the dangerous power from her body.

Malekith discovers that the Aether has returned and attacks Asgard. During 
the attack, Thor suffers a personal loss and decides to stop Malekith at any cost.

To defeat this powerful enemy, Thor asks his imprisoned brother Loki for help. 
Although Loki is known for his tricks and betrayal, Thor decides to trust him
for one last mission.

Together, Thor and Loki travel to the Dark World and face dangerous enemies. They
fight Malekith to prevent him from using the Aether during the Convergence, a 
rare cosmic event that connects the Nine Realms.

After an intense battle, Thor successfully defeats Malekith and saves the universe.
He returns to Asgard, where he makes an important decision about his future.

The story ends with Thor continuing his journey as a hero while Loki secretly
takes control of the throne of Asgard.

END OF STORY
