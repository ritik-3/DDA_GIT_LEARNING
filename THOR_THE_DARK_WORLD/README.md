# Thor: The Dark World (2013)

## 🎬 Movie Overview

**Thor: The Dark World** is a 2013 American superhero movie produced by Marvel Studios.
        It is the sequel to *Thor (2011)* and follows Thor's journey to protect the
        Nine Realms from an ancient enemy known as Malekith.

The movie combines action, adventure, fantasy, mythology, and science fiction.

## 📖 Story Description

After the events of *The Avengers*, Thor returns to Asgard to restore peace across the
Nine Realms. However, an ancient enemy named Malekith, the leader of the Dark Elves, 
awakens with a powerful weapon called the Aether.

Malekith plans to use the Aether to plunge the universe into eternal darkness. Meanwhile,
Jane Foster accidentally comes into contact with the Aether, putting her life in danger.

Thor takes Jane to Asgard to protect her and discovers that Malekith is preparing to 
attack. After a devastating attack on Asgard, Thor decides to stop Malekith at any cost.

To defeat this powerful enemy, Thor joins forces with his brother Loki, despite their 
complicated relationship.

Together, they travel to the Dark World and fight against Malekith during the Convergence,
a rare cosmic event connecting the Nine Realms.

Thor eventually defeats Malekith and saves the universe. However, the ending reveals an
unexpected twist involving Loki and the throne of Asgard.

## 🦸 Main Characters

- **Thor:** The God of Thunder and protector of Asgard.
- **Jane Foster:** An astrophysicist who becomes connected to the Aether.
- **Loki:** The God of Mischief and Thor's adopted brother.
- **Odin:** The King of Asgard and father of Thor.
- **Malekith:** The leader of the Dark Elves who wants to destroy the universe.
- **Frigga:** The Queen of Asgard and mother of Thor.
- **Heimdall:** The guardian of the Bifrost.
- **Sif:** A skilled Asgardian warrior and friend of Thor.
- **Erik Selvig:** A scientist who studies unusual cosmic events.
- **Darcy Lewis:** Jane Foster's assistant.
