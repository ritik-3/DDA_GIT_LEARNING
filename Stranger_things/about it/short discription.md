# Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is an American science-fiction and horror television series created by the Duffer Brothers. The story is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana.

The series follows a group of friends who discover mysterious events, secret experiments, supernatural powers, and a dangerous alternate dimension called the **Upside Down**. The main characters work together to protect their town and uncover the truth behind these strange events.

## Genre
- Science Fiction
- Horror
- Mystery
- Drama
- Thriller

## Main Themes
- Friendship and teamwork
- Mystery and adventure
- Supernatural events
- Courage and sacrifice
- Good vs. evil

**Stranger Things** is known for its 1980s setting, memorable characters, suspenseful story, and supernatural elements.
