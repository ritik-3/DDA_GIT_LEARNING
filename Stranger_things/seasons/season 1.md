# 📺 Seasons of Stranger Things

## 🔴 Season 1 – 2016

The story begins in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, when a young boy named **Will Byers mysteriously disappears**. His friends Mike, Dustin, and Lucas begin searching for him and encounter a mysterious girl named **Eleven**, who has extraordinary supernatural powers.

As they investigate, they discover a secret government laboratory and a terrifying alternate dimension known as the **Upside Down**. The season introduces the **Demogorgon** and establishes the mystery that connects Hawkins to the supernatural world.
