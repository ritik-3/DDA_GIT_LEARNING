# 📺 Seasons of Stranger Things

## 🟡 Season 3 – 2019

The third season takes place during the summer of 1985. The newly opened **Starcourt Mall** becomes an important location in the story. Beneath the mall, a secret operation is attempting to reopen the gateway to the Upside Down.

The **Mind Flayer** returns with a dangerous plan to control humans. The group faces new challenges while dealing with friendship, relationships, growing up, and the fight to save Hawkins.
