# 📺 Seasons of Stranger Things

## 🟠 Season 2 – 2017

After returning from the Upside Down, **Will Byers continues to experience strange visions and frightening connections** to the supernatural world. The group discovers that a much larger threat, known as the **Mind Flayer**, is controlling the Upside Down.

Meanwhile, Eleven learns more about her past and her abilities. The friends work together to protect Hawkins and try to close the connection between their world and the Upside Down.
