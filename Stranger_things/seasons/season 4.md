# 📺 Seasons of Stranger Things

## 🟢 Season 4 – 2022

In Season 4, the characters are separated across different locations while a terrifying new villain called **Vecna** begins attacking teenagers in Hawkins.

The group investigates Vecna's origins and discovers important connections between him, the **Upside Down**, and Eleven's past. The season becomes darker and more intense as the characters prepare for a major confrontation with Vecna.
