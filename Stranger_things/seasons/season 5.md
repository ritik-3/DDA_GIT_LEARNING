# 📺 Seasons of Stranger Things

## 🔵 Season 5 – 2025

The fifth and final season brings the story toward its ultimate conclusion. Hawkins faces the consequences of the events of the previous seasons, while the main characters reunite to confront the supernatural threat.

The final chapter focuses on **friendship, courage, sacrifice, and the fight to protect Hawkins**. The mysteries surrounding the Upside Down move toward their conclusion as the characters face their biggest challenge yet.
