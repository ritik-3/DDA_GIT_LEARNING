Sirius Black is an important character in the Harry Potter series and Harry Potter’s
godfather. He was a close friend of Harry’s parents, James and Lily Potter, during 
their time at Hogwarts. Sirius is a member of the Black family, a powerful wizarding
family, but he rejects their dark beliefs. He is wrongly accused of betraying Harry’s 
parents and spends years imprisoned in Azkaban. After escaping, Sirius reveals the truth
and becomes a loyal protector and father figure to Harry. He is known for his courage,
loyalty, bravery, and strong love for his friends and godson.
