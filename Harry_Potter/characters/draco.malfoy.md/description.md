Draco Malfoy is a major character in the Harry Potter series and a student of Slytherin 
House at Hogwarts. He comes from a wealthy and influential wizarding family and is the 
son of Lucius Malfoy and Narcissa Malfoy. Draco is initially shown as arrogant, proud, 
competitive, and often hostile toward Harry, Ron, and Hermione. He frequently tries to
compete with Harry and follows the beliefs of his family. As the story progresses,
Draco is forced to face difficult situations and struggles with the expectations placed 
on him. His character shows that people can be influenced by fear, family, and 
circumstances and are capable of making different choices.
