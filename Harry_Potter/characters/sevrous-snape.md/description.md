Severus Snape is an important character in the Harry Potter series and a professor
at Hogwarts. He teaches Potions and later becomes a Defence Against the Dark Arts
professor. Snape is known for his strict, serious, mysterious, and sometimes harsh
personality. He has a complicated past and a deep connection to Harry’s mother, 
Lily Potter. Although he often appears to dislike Harry, Snape secretly works toprotect him and plays an important role in the fight against Lord Voldemort. His 
character shows love, loyalty, sacrifice, and courage, making him one of the most 
complex characters in the series.
