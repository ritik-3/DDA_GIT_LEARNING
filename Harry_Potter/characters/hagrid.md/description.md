Rubeus Hagrid is a kind and loyal half-giant in the Harry Potter series. He works 
at Hogwarts and serves as the Keeper of Keys and Grounds. Hagrid is one of Harry’s
first friends in the wizarding world and helps him discover his magical heritage.
He deeply cares for magical creatures and often introduces Harry and his friends
to them. Despite his large and intimidating appearance, Hagrid is gentle, emotional,
brave, and extremely loyal to Dumbledore and Harry. He plays an important role 
throughout the series and always tries to protect his friends
