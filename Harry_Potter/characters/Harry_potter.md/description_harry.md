Harry Potter is the main protagonist of the Harry Potter series and a student of Gryffindor House at Hogwarts. He is the son of James and Lily Potter and becomes famous after surviving an attack by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort as a baby. Harry is known for his courage, loyalty, kindness, determination, and strong sense of justice. Along with his best friends Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, he faces many dangerous challenges and battles against dark forces. Harry eventually discovers the secrets behind Voldemort’s power and bravely faces him in the final battle, helping bring peace to the wizarding world.
