GRYFFINDORE

Gryffindor is one of the four houses at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
It was founded by Godric Gryffindor and is known for values such as bravery, courage,
determination, and chivalry. The house colors are scarlet and gold, and its symbol is
a lion. The head of Gryffindor is Professor Minerva McGonagall. Harry Potter, Ron Weasley,
Hermione Granger, and many other important characters belong to Gryffindor.
The house plays a major role throughout the Harry Potter story, especially during
the fight against Lord Voldemort.

Hufflepuff

Hufflepuff is one of the four houses at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
It was founded by Helga Hufflepuff and is known for loyalty, kindness, patience, hard
work, and fairness. The house colors are yellow and black, and its symbol is a badger. 
Hufflepuff values students who are hardworking, trustworthy, and willing to help others. 
Cedric Diggory and Nymphadora Tonks are among the well-known Hufflepuff characters


RAVENCLAW

Ravenclaw is one of the four houses at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. It was 
founded by Rowena Ravenclaw and is known for intelligence, wisdom, creativity, curiosity,
and learning. The house colors are blue and bronze, and its symbol is an eagle. Ravenclaw
students are encouraged to think independently, solve problems, and value knowledge. Luna
Lovegood, Cho Chang, and Filius Flitwick are some of the well-known Ravenclaw characters.

SLYTHERIN


Slytherin is one of the four houses at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. It was
founded by Salazar Slytherin and is known for ambition, determination, cleverness, resourcefulness,
and leadership. The house colors are green and silver, and its symbol is a snake. Slytherin students
are often ambitious and determined to achieve their goals. Draco Malfoy, Severus Snape, 
and Tom Riddle (Lord Voldemort) are some of the most well-known Slytherin characters.
