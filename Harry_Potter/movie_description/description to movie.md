Harry Potter is a fantasy movie series based on the novels by J.K. Rowling.
The story follows Harry Potter, a young orphan who discovers that he is a 
wizard and joins Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. At Hogwarts,
Harry becomes best friends with Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, and 
together they learn magic, explore the wizarding world, and face many
dangerous challenges. Harry discovers the truth about his parents and
learns that the dark wizard Lord Voldemort was responsible for their deaths. 
Throughout the series, Harry and his friends fight against dark forces and uncover
 secrets about Voldemort's past and powers. They eventually discover the Horcruxes, 
 which are connected to Voldemort's immortality, and work together to destroy them.
As the conflict grows, Hogwarts becomes the center of a great battle between good and evil. Harry ultimately faces Voldemort in a final confrontation and, through courage, friendship, love, and sacrifice, defeats him. The series ends with peace returning to the wizarding world and Harry beginning a new chapter of his life.
