1. The Beginning

Harry Potter is an orphan who lives with his aunt, uncle, and cousin. On his eleventh birthday, he discovers that he is a wizard and is invited to study at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

2. Life at Hogwarts

At Hogwarts, Harry meets Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, who become his best friends. They learn magic, attend classes, explore the castle, and experience many magical adventures together.

3. The Dark Secret

Harry learns that his parents were killed by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort. Voldemort also tried to kill Harry when he was a baby, but Harry survived the attack and became famous in the wizarding world.

4. The Fight Against Voldemort

As Harry grows older, Voldemort returns and becomes a major threat. Harry and his friends face dangerous creatures, dark magic, and many challenges while trying to protect Hogwarts and the wizarding world.

5. The Horcruxes

Harry, Ron, and Hermione discover that Voldemort has divided his soul into several objects called Horcruxes. They travel together to find and destroy them so that Voldemort can finally be defeated.

6. The Battle of Hogwarts

The conflict reaches its peak when Voldemort and his followers attack Hogwarts. Students, teachers, and members of the wizarding community fight together to defend the school.

7. The Final Battle

Harry finally faces Voldemort in a final confrontation. Through courage, friendship, love, and sacrifice, Harry defeats Voldemort and ends his threat to the wizarding world.

8. The Ending

After Voldemort's defeat, peace returns to the wizarding world. Harry, Ron, and Hermione move forward with their lives, carrying the memories and lessons of their journey at Hogwarts.




The Harry Potter movie series tells the story of Harry Potter, an orphan who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard. He begins studying at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he meets his best friends, Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. Together, they learn magic, make new friends, and uncover the secrets of the wizarding world. Harry soon discovers that the dark wizard Lord Voldemort killed his parents and tried to kill him when he was a baby, but Harry mysteriously survived. As Harry grows older, Voldemort returns and becomes a serious threat to the wizarding world. Harry, Ron, and Hermione face dangerous creatures, dark magic, and difficult challenges while searching for a way to defeat him. They discover that Voldemort has hidden parts of his soul in powerful objects called Horcruxes and set out to destroy them. Their journey leads to a major battle at Hogwarts, where many characters fight to protect the school and the wizarding world. In the final confrontation, Harry bravely faces Voldemort and defeats him. The story ends with the defeat of evil, the return of peace, and Harry and his friends beginning a new chapter in their lives.
