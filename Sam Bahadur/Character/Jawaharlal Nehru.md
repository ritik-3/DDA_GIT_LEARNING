 # Jawaharlal Nehru

## Portrayed By

**Played by:** Kunal Kapoor

## Character Overview

Jawaharlal Nehru is portrayed as the first Prime Minister of independent India. His character represents the political leadership of the newly formed nation during the early years after independence. In *Sam Bahadur*, he is shown as an important historical figure who was involved in shaping India's political and military environment during a crucial period of nation-building.

## Role in the Story

In the movie, Jawaharlal Nehru appears during the early phase of Sam Manekshaw's military career and the transition of the Indian Army after independence. His character provides historical context about the relationship between India's political leadership and the armed forces during the country's formative years.

The film highlights the challenges faced by the young nation and shows how military leaders like Sam Manekshaw operated under the guidance of India's political leadership. Although his role is brief, Nehru's presence helps establish the historical timeline and the political atmosphere in which Sam Manekshaw's career developed.

## Character Qualities

- Visionary national leader
- Strong believer in nation-building
- Diplomatic and thoughtful decision-maker
- Focused on India's development and security
- Supportive of institutional growth

## Significance in the Film

Although Jawaharlal Nehru has limited screen time in *Sam Bahadur*, his character is historically significant. He represents the leadership of early independent India and provides context for understanding the evolution of the Indian Army and the nation's journey during Sam Manekshaw's career.
