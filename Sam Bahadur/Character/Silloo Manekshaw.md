 # Silloo Manekshaw

## Portrayed By

**Sanya Malhotra**

## Character Overview

Silloo Manekshaw is portrayed as the **wife** of Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw in the movie *Sam Bahadur*. Her character shows the personal side of Sam's life beyond his military achievements.

She is shown as a loving, understanding, and supportive partner who stands beside Sam throughout his challenging journey as an Army officer.

## Role in the Story

In the movie, Silloo plays an important role in showing the personal sacrifices and emotional challenges faced by the family of a military officer.

As Sam's career demands frequent postings, responsibilities, and long periods away from home, Silloo remains a constant source of support and stability in his life. She understands Sam's dedication towards the Army and supports his commitment to serving the nation.

The film also highlights their relationship, where Silloo balances her own emotions while standing firmly with Sam during different phases of his military journey.

## Character Qualities

- Supportive and understanding partner
- Patient and emotionally strong
- Caring and compassionate
- Adaptable to the challenges of military life
- Strong pillar of support for Sam

## Significance in the Film

Although Silloo is not the main focus of the movie, her character adds emotional depth to Sam Manekshaw's story. She represents the sacrifices and strength of military families who support their loved ones behind the scenes.

Through Silloo's character, the film shows that behind every successful military leader, there is often a strong support system that helps them face challenges and continue their journey.
