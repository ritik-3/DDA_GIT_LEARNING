 # Pritam and Pedro

## Overview

**Pritam and Pedro** is a Hindi cyber-crime comedy-drama thriller set in Goa. The series follows two very different individuals who are brought together to investigate crimes involving the digital world. Their contrasting personalities, investigative styles, and unusual partnership create a story that combines **crime, mystery, technology, humour, and human emotions**.

The series stars **Arshad Warsi, Vir Hirani, and Vikrant Massey** in prominent roles and is produced by **Rajkumar Hirani Films**. It premiered on **JioHotstar on 3 July 2026** and consists of **6 episodes**.

## Story

The story revolves around **Pedro**, a seasoned police officer who relies on traditional investigative methods, and **Pritam**, a young and tech-savvy individual with strong knowledge of the digital world.

When a complex case involving cybercrime unfolds in Goa, the two are forced to work together despite their completely different approaches. Pedro trusts experience and instinct, while Pritam depends on technology and unconventional methods.

As they investigate the case, their partnership takes them through a series of unexpected situations, mixing **mystery, cybercrime, comedy, and suspense**. Their journey also highlights the growing risks associated with technology and the vulnerabilities created by an increasingly digital society.

## About the Series

*Pritam and Pedro* combines a cybercrime investigation with a light-hearted and character-driven narrative. Instead of presenting technology and crime as a purely dark thriller, the series uses humour and contrasting personalities to make the investigation more entertaining while still exploring the dangers of the digital world.
