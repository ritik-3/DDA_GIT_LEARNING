                                 Shashank Sharma

Played by: Siddhant Chaturvedi

Shashank Sharma is a kind-hearted and intelligent marketing professional from Patna who struggles with a speech impediment and deep self-doubt. Despite his talent and sincerity, his lack of confidence often prevents him from expressing himself and achieving his full potential. As he navigates the pressures of work, family expectations, and relationships, Shashank embarks on a journey of self-acceptance, discovering that true confidence comes from embracing his imperfections rather than hiding them.


Traits: Intelligent • Honest • Humble • Caring • Resilient • Introverted • Determined • Compassionate
