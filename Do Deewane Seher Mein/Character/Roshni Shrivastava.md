                                 Roshni Shrivastav

Played by: Mrunal Thakur

Roshni Shrivastav is a creative and independent woman working in Mumbai, quietly battling insecurities about her appearance and the constant comparisons made by society and her family. Strong-willed yet emotionally vulnerable, she values authenticity over perfection and gradually learns to accept herself for who she truly is. Through her journey with Shashank, she discovers the courage to embrace her individuality and believe in her own worth.


Traits: Independent • Creative • Compassionate • Practical • Emotionally Strong • Resilient • Honest • Self-aware
