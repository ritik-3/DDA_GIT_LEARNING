Season 1 of FROM introduces a mysterious town where anyone who enters becomes trapped. No matter which road they take, 
they eventually return to the same town.

1. The Matthews family arrives
- Jim, Tabitha, Julie and Ethan are travelling when they encounter a fallen tree.
- They take another road but repeatedly return to the same town.
- They discover that nobody can escape.

2. The town's biggest danger
- Strange human-looking creatures come out at night.
- They look normal, smile and talk to people.
- But they brutally kill anyone they can reach.
- Residents survive by staying indoors and using special protective stones called talismans.

3. Boyd's responsibility
- Boyd is the town's sheriff and leader.
- He tries to maintain peace and protect everyone.
- His son Ellis lives in Colony House.
- Boyd searches for answers and tries to find a way out.

4. Colony House vs Town
- Residents are divided into two communities:
  - Town: People who prefer a more organised and traditional lifestyle.
  - Colony House: People who prefer living together in a more relaxed community.
- Donna manages Colony House.

5. Sara's mysterious voices
- Sara starts hearing strange voices.
- The voices instruct her to kill certain people.
- She believes following them might help everyone escape.
- This creates a dangerous situation.


6. Victor's secrets
- Victor has lived in the town since childhood.
- He remembers strange events from the past.
- He has a mysterious connection with the Boy in White.
- His drawings contain clues about the town.

7. Jim and Jade's investigation
- Jim and Jade try to understand how the town works.
- They build a radio tower to contact someone outside.
- They successfully receive a mysterious response.


8. Tabitha's discovery
- Tabitha investigates underground tunnels.
- She discovers that the monsters sleep underground during the day.
- She also sees mysterious children who repeatedly say "Anghkooey."

9. Season 1 ending explained


Three major events happen:
1. The radio mystery: Jim receives a strange voice warning him that his wife should not be digging.
2. Tabitha's discovery: She reaches a mysterious lighthouse after falling into a hole.
3. A new arrival: A bus full of passengers enters the town, bringing even more people into danger.
Season 1 takeaway
Season 1 establishes three major mysteries:
- Why are people trapped in this town?
- Who or what controls the monsters?
- What is the connection between Victor, the Boy in White and the mysterious children?
