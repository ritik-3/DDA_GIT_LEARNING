# Slide 2: Introduction

Fast & Furious is one of the most popular action movie franchises in the world.
The series is famous for:

* High-speed car races
* Action sequences
* Family relationships
* Adventure and teamwork
* Luxury and modified cars

The franchise combines street racing culture with action and crime stories.

---

# Slide 3: History of Fast & Furious

* The first movie was released in 2001.
* The original movie focused on illegal street racing and car culture.
* Over time, the series changed into a global action adventure franchise.
* The movies became famous worldwide because of their action scenes and characters.
