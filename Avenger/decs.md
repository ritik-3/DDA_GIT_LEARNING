The Avengers is a superhero action film in which Earth's mightiest heroes—Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye—join forces for the first time. Recruited by Nick Fury and the organization S.H.I.E.L.D., they must stop Loki from leading an alien invasion of Earth using the powerful Tesseract. Despite their clashing personalities, the heroes learn to work together to save humanity in an epic battle for New York.
