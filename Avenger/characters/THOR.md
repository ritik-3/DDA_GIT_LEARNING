He is known for:

Wielding the enchanted hammer Mjolnir, which grants immense power and can only be lifted by those deemed worthy.
Controlling lightning, thunder, and storms.
Possessing superhuman strength, speed, durability, and a long lifespan.
Fighting to protect both Asgard and Earth from powerful threats.

Appearance:

Tall, muscular build.
Long blond hair (in many comic and movie versions).
Wears Asgardian armor with a flowing red cape.
Carries Mjolnir or, in some stories, the enchanted axe Stormbreaker.
