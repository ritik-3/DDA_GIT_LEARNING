Hulk and Bruce Banner -

Bruce Banner is a brilliant scientist who, after being exposed to massive amounts of gamma radiation,
gains the ability to transform into the incredibly powerful Hulk.

The Hulk is Bruce Banner’s powerful, green alter ego.
 He is extremely strong, durable, and becomes more powerful as his anger increases.

Their relationship
Bruce and Hulk are two sides of the same person. and bruce have 7 PHDs.
Bruce represents intelligence, calmness, and control,
while Hulk represents anger, strength, and raw emotion.

At first, Bruce sees Hulk as a dangerous curse, but over time, 
he learns to understand and accept him. Their relationship is a constant struggle between mind and emotion, control and power.
