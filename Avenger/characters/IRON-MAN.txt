He is known for:

Wearing a high-tech armored suit that gives him superhuman strength, flight, and advanced weapons.
His genius-level intelligence, which he uses to design and constantly improve his armor.
A confident, witty personality and strong leadership skills.
Using technology to protect the world rather than relying on natural superpowers.

Appearance:

Red and gold armored suit with a glowing chest-mounted Arc Reactor.
Helmet with a sleek metallic faceplate and glowing blue or white eyes.
Repulsor beams in his palms and powerful rocket boosters for flight.
