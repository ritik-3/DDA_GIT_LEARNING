She is a highly skilled spy, martial artist, and assassin who was trained in the secretive Red Room program. After leaving that life behind, she joined S.H.I.E.L.D. and became a trusted ally of heroes like Iron Man, Captain America, and Hawkeye.

Although Black Widow has no superhuman powers, she is known for:

Exceptional hand-to-hand combat skills
Expert espionage and infiltration abilities
Mastery of firearms and tactical weapons
High intelligence, quick thinking, and leadership under pressure
