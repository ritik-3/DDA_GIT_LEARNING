1. 🟣 Thanos — The Mad Titan
Introduction:
Thanos is one of the most powerful villains in the MCU.
He is an Eternal from Titan who believes that the universe's resources are limited and that destroying half of all life will restore balance.

Movies: The Avengers (2012), Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015),
Avengers: Infinity War (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019)

Powers:

Superhuman strength and durability
Extremely advanced combat skills
Genius-level intelligence
Immense stamina
Infinity Stones when equipped with the Infinity Gauntlet
How powerful? — 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 10/10

Example:
Without using the Infinity Stones, Thanos defeated Hulk in hand-to-hand combat.
With all six Infinity Stones, he could manipulate space, time, reality, energy, minds, and souls.

Ultimate feat: He snapped his fingers and erased half of all life in the universe.
