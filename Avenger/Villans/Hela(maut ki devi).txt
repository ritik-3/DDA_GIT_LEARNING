⚔️ Hela — Goddess of Death
Introduction:
Hela is Odin's firstborn daughter and the former ruler of Asgard's armies.

Movie: Thor: Ragnarok (2017)

Powers:

Incredible superhuman strength
Regeneration
Immortality-like longevity
Creates enormous weapons from her body
Can summon countless blades
Extremely powerful combatant
How powerful? — 🔥🔥🔥🔥 9/10

Example:
Hela catches and destroys Mjolnir with one hand.

She also defeats Thor in combat and destroys Asgard's armies.

Ultimate weakness: Her power is strongly connected to Asgard.
Destroying Asgard ultimately becomes necessary to stop her.
