 Surtur — The Agent of Ragnarok
Movie: Thor: Ragnarok
Origin: Muspelheim
Role: Fire demon
Power Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 9.5/10

Introduction
Surtur is an ancient fire demon whose destiny is connected to Ragnarok—the destruction of Asgard.

Main Motivation
Fulfill Ragnarok.
Destroy Asgard.
Complete his destined role.
Powers
Massive physical strength
Fire manipulation
Immense durability
Giant size
Eternal Flame enhancement
Massive flaming sword
Major Example
After gaining the Eternal Flame, Surtur becomes enormous and destroys Asgard itself.

Sacrifice
Surtur's story is less emotional than Gorr or Loki. His "sacrifice" is accepting his role in Ragnarok and essentially using his own existence as the instrument of destruction.

Why He Is Wrong
He is not morally complex in the same way as Loki or Gorr. His purpose is fundamentally destructive, but his philosophy can be interpreted as destruction being necessary for renewal.
