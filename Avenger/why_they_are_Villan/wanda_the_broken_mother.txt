 Scarlet Witch — The Broken Mother-

Movie/Series: Avengers: Age of Ultron, Civil War, Infinity War, Endgame, WandaVision, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness
Origin: Sokovia
Role: Hero → antagonist
Power Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 10/10

Introduction-
Wanda Maximoff starts as an antagonist but eventually becomes an Avenger.
Her immense magical potential makes her one of the most dangerous beings in the MCU.

Main Motivation-
Her actions are heavily influenced by:

Loss of her parents
Death of Pietro
Loss of Vision
Desire for a family
Desire for her children
Grief and loneliness

Powers-
Chaos magic
Telekinesis
Telepathy
Reality manipulation
Energy projection
Mind manipulation
Magical shields
Extremely powerful destructive magic

Major Examples-
Overpowered several powerful opponents.
Destroyed Vision's Mind Stone while simultaneously holding Thanos back.
Nearly defeated Thanos by herself.
In Multiverse of Madness, she becomes a major threat even to powerful sorcerers.

Sacrifice-
Her biggest sacrifice is her own happiness.

In WandaVision, she creates a world where she has Vision and children. But eventually she understands that her happiness is hurting innocent people.

She lets it all go.

Why She Is Wrong-
Her grief is understandable, but she starts believing that her pain gives her the right to control or harm others.
