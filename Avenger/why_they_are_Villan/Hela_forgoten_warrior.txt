Hela — The Forgotten Warrior-

Movie: Thor: Ragnarok
Origin: Asgard
Role: Goddess of Death
Power Level: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 9/10

Introduction-
Hela is Odin's firstborn and the former leader of Asgard's armies.
She helped build Asgard's empire through conquest.

Main Motivation-

Believes Asgard abandoned her.
Wants her rightful place.
Wants to restore Asgard's former glory.
Feels erased from history.
Powers
Superhuman strength
Immense durability
Regeneration
Weapon creation
Necromancy-like abilities
Creates blades and weapons from her body
Exceptional combat skills
Major Examples
Catches and destroys Mjolnir.
Defeats Thor.
Easily defeats Asgardian soldiers.
Takes control of Asgard.
Sacrifice
Hela believes she sacrificed her youth, loyalty and life for Asgard's expansion, only to be imprisoned and erased from its history.

Why She Is Wrong-
Her anger has understandable roots, but she chooses domination and violence rather than accepting that Asgard's values have changed.
