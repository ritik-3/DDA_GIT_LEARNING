 'System' is a courtroom drama and legal thriller that explores the true meaning of justice in a world where power and influence often decide the outcome of legal cases. The story follows "Neha Rajvansh", a determined public prosecutor who wants to prove that she is a capable lawyer without relying on her father's reputation. Her father, "Ravi Rajvansh", is a famous senior lawyer who challenges her to win ten court cases before she can join his law firm. During this journey, Neha meets "Sarika Rawat", a humble courtroom stenographer whose sharp observation skills and years of courtroom experience help Neha solve complex cases. As they investigate one case after another, they uncover hidden connections, corruption, and long-buried truths within the legal system. The story eventually leads to a powerful courtroom battle where Neha must choose between family loyalty and justice. Through its suspenseful plot and emotional moments, 'System' highlights themes of truth, integrity, equality, and the importance of standing up against a flawed system.
