 Sarika Rawat (Jyothika) 
 is a humble and hardworking courtroom stenographer who comes from a modest background. She supports her family while taking care of her disabled husband and daughter. 
 
 Despite not being a lawyer, Sarika has a sharp mind and years of courtroom experience, allowing her to notice important details that others often miss. Her intelligence and practical knowledge help Neha solve several complex cases. As they work together, Sarika becomes an essential part of Neha's journey toward justice. Her character represents resilience, wisdom, compassion, and the belief that true intelligence comes from experience as well as education.
