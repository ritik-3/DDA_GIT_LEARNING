 Ravi Rajvansh (Ashutosh Gowariker) 
 is a highly respected and successful senior lawyer as well as Neha's father. He is intelligent, disciplined, and believes that success should be earned through hard work rather than family connections. 
 
 To test Neha's abilities, he challenges her to win ten court cases before joining his law firm. Although he appears strict and demanding, his actions are driven by his desire to make Neha a strong and independent lawyer. Throughout the story, Ravi's beliefs and relationship with his daughter are put to the test as they face each other in court. His character represents discipline, experience, high standards, and the conflict between family loyalty and professional ethics.
