 Neha Rajvansh (Sonakshi Sinha) 
 is the main character of 'System'. She is an ambitious and talented public prosecutor who wants to prove that she is capable on her own rather than relying on her father's reputation. Although she comes from a privileged background, Neha believes in justice and works hard to earn her success. 
 
 She teams up with Sarika Rawat, whose courtroom experience helps her solve difficult cases. As the story progresses, Neha faces several moral and professional challenges that test her courage and integrity. By the end of the film, she chooses to fight for truth and justice instead of following power or family expectations. Neha represents determination, honesty, and the courage to stand up for what is right.
