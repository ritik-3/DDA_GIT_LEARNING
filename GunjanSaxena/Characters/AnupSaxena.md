 Pankaj Tripathi as Anup Saxena (Gunjan's Father)

 Character Description:

    Anup Saxena is a retired Indian Army officer and Gunjan's biggest supporter.
    He always encourages her to follow her dream, even when society questions her ambitions.
    He teaches her confidence, courage, and the importance of believing in herself.
    His guidance and emotional support play a major role in helping Gunjan overcome obstacles.
    

Personality Traits:

    Supportive
    Wise
    Caring
    Motivating
    Patient
    Encouraging
