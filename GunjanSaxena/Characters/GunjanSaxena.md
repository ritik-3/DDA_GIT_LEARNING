 Janhvi Kapoor as Gunjan Saxena

 Character Description:

    Gunjan Saxena is a determined, fearless, and hardworking young woman whose dream is to become an Indian Air Force pilot.
    She faces gender bias, self-doubt from others, and many challenges but never gives up on her goal.
    She believes that dedication, discipline, and courage are more important than stereotypes.
    During the Kargil War, she proves her bravery by flying rescue and supply missions in dangerous conditions, earning the respect of her colleagues.


Personality Traits:

    Brave
    Determined
    Confident
    Disciplined
    Resilient
    Compassionate
