 Manav Vij as Commanding Officer / Mentor

 Character Description:

    Gunjan's commanding officer is a strict but fair mentor who expects professionalism and discipline from every cadet.
    He initially evaluates Gunjan based on her performance rather than sympathy.
    As she proves her capability through hard work and determination, he begins to respect her   and trusts her with important responsibilities.
    His training helps Gunjan become a stronger and more confident Air Force pilot.
